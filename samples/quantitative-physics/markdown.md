# Newton's Laws of Motion — Teacher Knowledge Package

*Physics | Grade 9-10 | intermediate*

**Chapter:** Chapter 5

**Source:** Newton's Laws of Motion

## Learning Objectives

- [understand] State Newton's first law and identify it acting in everyday situations.
- [apply] Calculate the acceleration of a body given net force and mass.

## Teaching Plan

### Period 1: Inertia and the First Law

- **Concepts:** Inertia
- **Time:** Entry ticket (5 min), Direct instruction (15 min), Activity (12 min), Checkpoint (5 min), Exit ticket (3 min)
- **Rationale:** Inertia is the prerequisite for the second law, so it is taught first.

### Period 2: Force, Mass and Acceleration

- **Concepts:** Newton's Second Law
- **Time:** Entry ticket (5 min), Derivation (15 min), Problem set (12 min), Checkpoint (5 min), Exit ticket (3 min)
- **Rationale:** Builds quantitatively on the qualitative idea of inertia.

## Formulae

- **Newton's Second Law**: `\vec{F} = m\vec{a}` — F equals m times a

## Classroom Content

### Period 1

- **Entry ticket:** Name one situation where something kept moving after you stopped pushing it.
  - 0-5 min: Entry ticket
  - 5-20 min: Inertia and the First Law
  - 20-32 min: Activity
  - 32-37 min: Checkpoint
  - 37-40 min: Exit ticket
- **Activities:** Coin and card
- **Exit ticket:** In one sentence, why do passengers lurch forward when a bus brakes?

### Period 2

- **Entry ticket:** Name one situation where something kept moving after you stopped pushing it.
  - 0-5 min: Entry ticket
  - 5-20 min: Force, Mass and Acceleration
  - 20-32 min: Activity
  - 32-37 min: Checkpoint
  - 37-40 min: Exit ticket
- **Activities:** Computing acceleration
- **Exit ticket:** In one sentence, why do passengers lurch forward when a bus brakes?

## Assessment — Questions

1. (1 marks) A bus brakes suddenly and passengers lurch forward. Why?
   - A. A forward force acts on them
   - B. They continue moving while the bus slows
   - C. Gravity increases during braking
   - D. Air pressure pushes them forward
2. (3 marks) A 4 kg trolley experiences a net force of 12 N. Find its acceleration.

## Assessment — Answer Key

*Teacher copy — not for distribution to students.*

1. B. They continue moving while the bus slows
2. 3 m/s^2

## Learning Gaps & Misconceptions

- **[high]** Motion requires a continuously applied force.
  - Remediation: Contrast a low-friction and a high-friction surface side by side.
